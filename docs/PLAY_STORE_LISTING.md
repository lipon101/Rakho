## Full description

**English:**

> Your pharmacy loses money two ways: medicine that expires on the shelf, and
> baki that is never written down. Rakho fixes both — and it's free to start.
>
> ⏰ EXPIRY RADAR
> Every batch's expiry date is tracked. Rakho shows what expires this month —
> with the taka value at risk — and blocks expired medicine from being sold
> by mistake. Sell the nearest-expiry batch first (FEFO) automatically.
>
> 🤝 BAKI BOOK (বাকির খাতা)
> Sell on credit with the customer's name. See who owes what, since when.
> Record payment in one tap and the balance updates instantly. Nothing
> forgotten, nothing argued about at the counter.
>
> 🧾 FAST BILLING
> Cash, bKash, Nagad, card and baki in one screen. Discounts, change due and
> baki remainder calculated for you. Invoices are numbered automatically —
> even offline.
>
> 📶 WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
> Load-shedding or no network? Sell, receive stock and check expiry without
> internet. Everything syncs safely when the connection returns.
>
> 💊 14,000+ BANGLADESH MEDICINES
> Connect your Rakho key to search the national catalog — brand, generic,
> strength and manufacturer — while adding stock. No typing, no spelling
> mistakes. (Works with manual entry too.)
>
> 📊 KNOW YOUR SHOP
> Today's sales, real profit (not just revenue), stock value at cost, low
> stock, expiry risk — plus CSV reports you can share with anyone.
>
> 🔒 PRIVATE AND SECURE
> Your records stay on your device unless you connect a key. API keys are
> stored hashed, traffic is encrypted, and there are no ads or trackers.
>
> 🌍 বাংলা ও ENGLISH
> Full Bangla interface. Built in Bangladesh for Bangladeshi pharmacies —
> and useful for any medicine retailer, anywhere in the world.
>
> Free forever for the core features. Rakho Pro adds cloud sync, the national
> medicine catalog and multi-device support.

## Keywords / ASO notes

  `inventory`, `ফার্মেসি`, `ওষুধ`, `বাকি`

- Localize the listing to `bn-BD` in the console (translate listing page).
